    Would it have worked if you only knew the past?

    A backtest can look good because its settings were tuned on the same data. Walk-forward removes that. Each year, settings are chosen only from the previous 3 years and then traded on a year the choice never saw.

    Foundations

    How day trading NQ futures works

    A day trader opens and closes every position within one session, so nothing is held overnight. The edge is small, so costs, position size and discipline decide whether you keep any of it.

    Read context

    Before 9:30: overnight high/low, prior day high/low/close, economic calendar (8:30, 10:00, 2:00 ET).

    Pick a tested setup

    Trade only setups with a backtested edge. On NQ that means momentum and breakouts, not fades. See the Lab.

    Size from the stop

    Put the stop where the idea is wrong, then size so that stop costs ≤ 1% of the account.

    Bracket every entry

    Enter with the stop (and target, if the setup has one) already attached. No negotiating with losers.

    Journal & compare

    Log each trade. The Journal compares your stats with the backtest for that setup.

    Market mechanics

    The order book and the spread

    Bids rest below the market and offers rest above it. A market order fills immediately against the other side. A limit order waits at your price. A stop order turns into a market order when price touches it. NQ is usually one tick (0.25 pts) wide.
